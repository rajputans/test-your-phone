    HOW THE SIGNALS ARE COLLECTED

    Signals you can
    actually verify.

    DeviceFit measures DNS, TLS and response-header timings, then runs real Chromium against a bounded, SSRF-checked network bridge. CPU and network throttling model broad phone, tablet and laptop scenarios; they do not test physical devices.

    Browser paint/layout/resource metrics, responsive viewports, screenshots and automated axe-core findings are produced live. Device-class fit is an estimate derived from those measurements, not a model-specific recommendation. A server-side HTTP 405 means that probe method was rejected; it does not, by itself, mean the site is broken.

    LIMITATIONS & PRIVACY
    01

    Emulation, not a device lab

    Chromium estimates phone/tablet/laptop-class website fit under emulated CPU and network conditions. No physical device or live retail-price feed is used.

    02

    Not a security audit

    Headers, TLS and optional threat-list signals do not detect every threat or prove a site is safe. Google Safe Browsing receives the submitted URL only after explicit opt-in and provider configuration.

    03

    Minimal collection

    Submitted URLs, measurements and temporary screenshots stay in process memory for up to one hour. Page content and cookie values are not stored. Restarting the service clears reports.
